Welcome to the Gallery Printshop! The background image file needs to be printed. Clear a color set by having them fall and touch in any grouping of four. Each color you clear adds that color plate to the image. The goal is to add every color to the image and "print" it. The side tubes empty as you fill the image to track your progress of that color.

Beware of virus colors that cannot match and randomize colors they touch. You need to combo clear (one set after another) or match six of one color to clear viruses.

Magnetic ink has a gold shine and works like the others, but it also drags a matching drop across to sit beside it.

The Gallery is twenty paintings in six chapters, printed in order. Each painting you print is a level.

Every level is harder than the one before it. The drops fall faster, each color takes more clears to fill, and new inks join the set along the way.

The board ranks you by levels printed. Total time only breaks a tie, and each level counts at your best time on it, so going back to beat one can only move you up.

Sign in on the arcade page to keep your place between visits.
