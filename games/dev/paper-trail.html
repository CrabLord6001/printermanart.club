Paper Trail

Ms. Printer: Paper Trail

How to Play

  • WASD, arrow keys, or swipe
  • Consume ink to keep printing
  • Hit your own printouts or the wall and it’s game over

PowerUps

  • Magnet attract ink
  • Phasing pass through objects
  • Time Warp slow movement
