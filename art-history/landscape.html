Landscape + Nature

2 works

Wheat Fields

| Jacob van Ruisdael
iCatalogue details

Dutch, 1628/29–1682 · ca. 1670 · Oil on canvas, 100 × 130.2 cm
The Metropolitan Museum of Art, 14.40.623 · Public domain · metmuseum.org

More than half the canvas is given to the clouds, and the land below is arranged to serve them: the track widens as it comes forward, and the two figures on it are small enough to set the scale of everything else. The wheat is pale and nearly ready to cut. Ruisdael and his generation worked the horizon this low so that weather could be the real subject of a landscape.

Shown 23 October 2026

Twilight in the Wilderness

| Frederic Edwin Church
iCatalogue details

American, 1826–1900 · 1860 · Oil on canvas, 101.6 × 162.6 cm
The Cleveland Museum of Art, 1965.233 · Public domain (CC0) · clevelandart.org

Painted the year before the Civil War began, from studies Church made in Maine, and emptied of every human figure — no cabin, no boat, no road. Almost half the canvas is sky. The red is laid in thin glazes over a pale ground so the clouds appear lit from behind rather than colored on the surface.

Shown 19 October 2026
