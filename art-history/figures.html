Madame X (Virginie Amélie Avegno Gautreau)

| John Singer Sargent
iCatalogue details

American, 1856–1925 · 1883–84 · Oil on canvas, 208.6 × 109.9 cm
The Metropolitan Museum of Art, 16.53 · Public domain · metmuseum.org

The 1884 Paris Salon received it so badly that Sargent left for London and stayed. Gautreau powdered her skin with lavender, and Sargent matched it exactly, which is why she reads almost gray against the black dress and brown ground. The right strap originally hung off her shoulder; he repainted it upright after the Salon, and the picture stayed in his studio for thirty years.
