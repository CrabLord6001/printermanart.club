The blockchain intelligence firms TRM Labs and Chainalysis said they were highly confident that the North Korean hacking unit TraderTraitor, which investigators link to the Lazarus Group, carried out the attack, using a method that mirrored the 2025 theft at Bybit that the FBI attributed to Pyongyang; North Korea routinely denies such claims and its missions in Geneva and China did not answer questions from Reuters. Bitcoin fell to a six-week low of $76,368 and ether slipped more than 3 percent to $2,178, while shares in Coinbase, which said it was unaffected, closed down 4.8 percent. Ari Redbord, TRM’s global head of policy, called the attack unprecedented; the previous record was the $1.5 billion taken from Bybit last year.

Patrick Stewart Dies at 86

The Royal Shakespeare veteran who became Picard and Professor X had Alzheimer’s and Lewy body dementia

Los AngelesPatrick Stewart died on Wednesday at his home in Los Angeles at the age of 86, his family said in a statement posted to Instagram the following day, attributing his death to Alzheimer’s disease and Lewy body dementia and describing a brief and poignant journey with the illness. Born in Mirfield, Yorkshire, in 1940, he trained at the Bristol Old Vic theatre school and spent 16 years with the Royal Shakespeare Company, winning two Olivier Awards, before taking the role of Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek: The Next Generation in 1987 and returning to it in Star Trek: Picard in 2020. He played Professor Charles Xavier in the X-Men films from 2000 and again in Logan and Deadpool & Wolverine, won a Grammy in 1996 for a recording of Peter and the Wolf, drew four Emmy nominations, and was knighted for services to drama. The family statement praised his dignity, compassion and humanity and his work on human rights, domestic violence, refugees, veterans’ mental health and animal welfare; he is survived by his wife, Sunny Ozell, and by a son and a daughter.
